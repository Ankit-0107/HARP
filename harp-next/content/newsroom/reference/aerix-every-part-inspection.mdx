Aerix machines titanium parts where a fraction of a millimeter decides whether a component flies. Their old process measured a sample and trusted the rest.

## Why sampling wasn't enough

Audit requirements and the cost of a single bad part meant Aerix needed a record for every unit, not a statistical estimate of quality.

## Inspection on the line

Precept integrated machine vision and measurement directly into production. Each part is checked against spec and logged the moment it is made.

## The outcome

First-article approvals that once took days now clear in hours, and every part ships with traceable proof it met tolerance.
